## Recovery changes

The accepted diagnosis from `reports/codex/260909_codex_04.md` was confirmed.
The earlier task-local verifier incorrectly required successful MCP responses
to omit `isError`; `rmcp 2.2.0` may serialize success as `isError: false`.
Production code, design, transport, Bot/TCC state, Feishu, notifications, HOME
migration, and unrelated UI/configuration were not changed.

Recovery used one task-local standard-library verifier under the project
scratch boundary. It classifies only `isError: true` or a protocol error as
failure. For a successful result it extracts only `request_id`,
`selected_choice_id`, and `source_channel_id`, validates those values against a
safe identifier/channel boundary, writes them to an owner-only temporary file,
fsyncs the file, and only then performs request-specific assertions and process
teardown. The verifier and its evidence are not tracked release content.
