After bootstrap, normal build/test/worker health/MCP/message operations should require no administrator password unless a new real system-level change is introduced.

If privilege is no longer valid during the bounded bootstrap, stop with one explicit `ADMIN_AUTH_REQUIRED` checkpoint rather than creating repeated prompts.
