The same blocker reproduced after restoring released v0.1.2, proving it was pre-existing rather than caused by the decimal/link patch.

This task does **not** patch disabled-Feishu secret loading. It removes Feishu from the product so the runtime has no reason to know, load, decrypt, migrate, dispatch, configure, document, or test that channel.
